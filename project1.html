Overview

Math Quiz is an Android app that drills multiplication and division facts across the 1–12 times tables. Problems are presented one at a time using a custom numeric keypad, and the app evaluates the answer immediately on submission. It's designed to be approachable for younger learners but works just as well for adults brushing up on mental math.

A voice toggle lets users enable spoken feedback powered by the Android Web Speech API's speech synthesis interface — delivering encouraging responses for correct answers and gentle, supportive redirects for incorrect ones, rather than a simple right/wrong indicator.

What I Built & Learned

The app uses the Web Speech API's SpeechSynthesis interface to generate spoken feedback on demand, triggered by answer evaluation and controlled by a user-facing voice on/off toggle. Problems are randomized across the full 1–12 multiplication and division range on each new question.

Building it reinforced my understanding of JavaScript event handling, DOM manipulation, and how to interface with a device's native speech capabilities through a web-based layer. Packaging the whole thing as an installable Android app — with its own home screen icon — was a satisfying way to see a self-contained JavaScript project ship as something that feels like a real app.
